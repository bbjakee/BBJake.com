About Jake

Jacob Coburn, better known as BBJake is the 559's #1 content creator and the face of Visalia streaming. Streamer, YouTuber and event host, he built his following by showing up for the community, online and in person, every time.

He's live almost every day, and has already collabed with some of the biggest names, including Adin Ross, Richard Torrez, Deen The Great, Yvng Ecko, and was even featured on SportsCenter. The 559 is where his heart is, and the city shows up for him because he shows up for the city!

Jake is giving back with food drives, giveaways and events that bring locals together. Boxing events, car meets, football tournaments and the biggest water balloon event in the 559 help strengthen the Visalia community.

In his latest video, he took a homeless man on the trip of a lifetime for 48 hours! Watch it here on YouTube.

Jake is also the CEO of BeenBlessed, a Christian clothing brand out of Visalia. With the largest audience in the area and a loyal local fanbase that actually shows up, Jake puts your brand in front of the people in the 559 who care. Let's talk.
