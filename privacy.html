Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. The short version: the site does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sell your information.

What we collect

This site does not have accounts, sign-ups or contact forms, and we do not ask you for personal information to browse it. If you email info@bbjake.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and understand general traffic.

Embedded and linked services

  • The live stream and chat are provided by Twitch.
  • Videos are provided by YouTube.
  • Fonts are provided by Google Fonts.
  • Links go to Instagram, TikTok, Discord and the BeenBlessed Brand store.

When these services load on your device, they may set cookies or collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control that, so please review their policies.

Merchandise purchases

If you buy something, you do it on the BeenBlessed Brand store at beenblessedbrand.com. Your name, address and payment details go to that store and its payment processor, not to this site.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Cookies that come from embedded third-party services are covered above.

How information is used and shared

We use the limited information we receive to run the site and answer messages. We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers that help run the site, or if the law requires it.

Children

The site is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence we hold from you by writing to info@bbjake.com. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, though embedded players may not work without them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest change. Questions: info@bbjake.com.
